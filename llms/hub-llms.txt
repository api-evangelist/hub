# Hub.xyz

> Hub.xyz is a physical-AI data company. It creates, annotates and curates custom real-world datasets — egocentric, video, image and audio — used to train AI and robotics models, sourced through a global network of retail contributors and verified small and medium businesses.

Hub.xyz turns real-world human activity into machine training signal. Contributors and partner businesses capture first-person and multimodal recordings of everyday physical work; Hub curates, annotates and labels them into pipeline-ready datasets. Hub offers both off-the-shelf datasets and bespoke, custom-scoped data collection projects quoted within hours.

## Datasets

- Egocentric: first-person POV video of workplace and residential tasks, with IMU data, RGB depth and rigged 3D hand reconstruction.
- Image: diverse raw and professionally edited images across dozens of verticals.
- Video: multi-object tracking, action segmentation and scene-level captions, frame-accurate at 30fps.
- Audio: multilingual field recordings with speaker diarization, language and accent tags, and per-utterance emotion labels.

## Offerings

- Off-the-shelf datasets: structured, labeled and annotated data, ready for training.
- Bespoke collection: custom-scoped projects priced by modality, volume, coverage and complexity, with a quote returned within hours and a fully managed end-to-end pipeline.
- Verified SMB network: a contracted, capture-ready network of small and medium businesses across many industries.

## Business partner program

Hub pays small and medium businesses for egocentric recordings of the work their teams already do. A head-mounted camera captures a first-person view of hands at work (session-based, no audio); Hub reviews the footage and turns it into training data for robotics and physical AI.

- Who it's for: SMBs across 18 physical-work environments — restaurant and commercial kitchens, warehouses and distribution, construction sites, plumbing/HVAC/electrical, auto service, metal fabrication and welding, commercial cleaning, hotels, food and beverage plants, and more.
- Earnings: US$3–10 per approved hour of recorded work, with $0 hardware cost, no minimum volume, and setup in under an hour.
